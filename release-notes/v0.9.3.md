# GRAIL'D 0.9.3

- Redesigned interface across the Hub, Game, Catalog, Leaderboard, Settings, and Account screens, with clearer empty, loading, and error states.
- New challenge modes: Sudden Death, Three Lives, Time Attack (90 seconds), and Sprint (10 songs). Signed-in players keep a personal best and run count for each mode.
- Game: each round shows how many points it is worth, a timeline of your attempts (wrong, skipped, current), a log of the guesses you made, and your recent rounds.
- Catalog: search your library, filter by Canary era status, sort A–Z or by most-missed songs, and switch between grid and list layouts.
- Leaderboard: sort your per-song records, and see song insights such as your most-missed tracks.
- Animations respect the system "reduce motion" setting.
- Google sign-in now uses verified Google ID tokens, matching the hardened sign-in server.
- Existing accounts, achievements, saved rounds, settings, and music folders remain in their existing user data locations.
